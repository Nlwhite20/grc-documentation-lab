# GRC Documentation Lab Rules

Personal, authorized home-lab project. This is a **self-assessment** by the lab owner: not an
audit, not an attestation, and not a claim of compliance or certification with any framework.

## Source material

- Third-party documents (AICPA Trust Services Criteria, the AICPA TSC-to-NIST mapping, CSA CAIQ)
  stay on the owner's Mac in `~/home-labs/grc-sources/` and are **never committed**.
- Do not copy criteria or questionnaire text. Reference items **by ID** and describe them in our
  own words; short quotations only where clearly fair use, with attribution.
- NIST SP 800-53 control names may be quoted (US government publication).

## Evidence

- Every "Implemented" or "Partial" status must link to evidence in a public lab repository.
- If evidence is missing, the status is "Not implemented" or "Not assessed", never assumed.
- No real IP addresses, usernames or credentials.

## Approval

Ask before committing, pushing, or changing any lab system. This lab changes documents only.
